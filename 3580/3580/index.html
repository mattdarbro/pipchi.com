Tallwood's note

On the table when we opened the shop.

Matt wrote this one in 2012 — and wrote it like he used to. Lyrics first, then synths, then guitars. The themes are the ones a fourteen-year-old works through: house, distance, kings, hope. The writer is older. The themes hold up anyway.

The gear is a 2012 idea of 1985. 707 drums. Akai synths. Compression. Room treatments meant to feel like the basement at the original 3580 — an actual house, before it was a shop name. Not retro for retro's sake. Lo-fi in the classic sense.

It opens with a haunted instrumental and closes on a song called Hope. In between, it remembers a cousin and a song that's been in this catalog, in one form or another, since 1986.

Tallwood's note
Liner · Track 11 · "Slight Variation #3" lineage, 1986–

Slight Variation #3 was written by Matt and a friend in 1986. From then on, whenever they opened a studio in a new room — anywhere — that song was the first thing played. The 1-2-3 to test the space.

There are four or five recordings of it in the boxes. This one's reflective. It's a song about writing music. It's a letter to a younger Matt to keep writing.
