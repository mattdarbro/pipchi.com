Bleach

A Musical Witness. I threaded it up in the back and left it running.

Tallwood's note

Some things you can't put on a record.

A record shop isn't only records. Now and then a thing arrives that has to be watched. So I keep a projector in the back, and when there's a picture worth running, it goes up on the wall here. This one is called Bleach — a musical witness.

It's the first reel down the lanes. Same idea as everything else in the shop: don't just hand you the thing, tell you about it. Sit with it. Let it run twice if you need to.

— filed by Tallwood, projection booth
