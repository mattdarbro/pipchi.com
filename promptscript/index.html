Pre-production with a crew that talks back. Develop the vision before a single frame is generated.

Every film starts with a vision. Prompt Script gives you a creative crew — division by division, consultant by consultant — to develop that vision out loud. You leave with a shot-ready script the camera can chase.

⏵ Open in the App Store $4.99 / month · Free to try
The recipe

Three rooms, one shooting script.

01

Audition

Talk to the consultants. Each one is a director's brain in a specific mode — Firebrand for kinetic energy, Gothic Visionary for shadowed romance, Dreamer for soft light and slow time. Switch between them mid-conversation.

02

Divisions

Break your story into the bones — settings, characters, divisions, beats. Each gets its own room to develop in. Your consultant sees the whole.

03

Prompt Script

Generate a shot-by-shot script with SCRIPT · VISUAL · AUDIO · EDIT tracks. Hand it to your AI generator of choice. The camera can chase what's on the page.
