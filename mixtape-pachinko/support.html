A PIPCHI RECORD · SIDE A Mixtape Pachinko

Support

Mixtape Pachinko is a toy more than a game: a record spinning under a pachinko board, where every ball that hits a peg plays a piece of the song. There's no score to chase. Drop the needle and play.

How to play

  • DRAG THE RECORDScratch it. Spin it backward, slow it down, fling it.
  • SWIPE LEFT / RIGHTOff the record: change the board, the layout of pegs.
  • SWIPE UP / DOWNChange the song. Sixteen records in the crate.
  • DRAIN BALLS INTO ✳ # % @The four zones at the bottom add reverb, hi-pass, lo-pass and delay.
  • LATCHHold the loop that's playing.
  • PACH / MIXPachinko mode builds the song from loops. Mixtape mode plays the whole record and you play over it.
  • RECRecord what you hear. Tap stop to share it.
  • DROP RATEHalf, normal or double the balls.

Pegs near the rim play the song's baseline material; pegs closer to the center reach the hook. Where the balls land is how you steer the music.

Questions

Where do my recordings go?

When you tap stop, the share sheet opens with your take as an audio file named after the song. Save it to Files, AirDrop it, or send it in Messages. The app doesn't keep a copy, so save the ones you love.

Can I turn off the voices?

Yes. Open SETTINGS (top-left corner, or on the title card) and switch off Callouts.

Can I skip the title card?

Switch off Title card at launch in Settings.

The music plays with my phone on silent.

That's on purpose: it's an instrument, so the silent switch doesn't mute it. Use the volume buttons.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything, including all sixteen songs, lives in the app and works offline.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

I found a bug.

Please email us what happened, which song and board you were on, and your iPhone model. A screen recording helps a lot.
