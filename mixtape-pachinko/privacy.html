A PIPCHI RECORD · SIDE A Mixtape Pachinko

Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026.

The short version

Mixtape Pachinko collects no data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your iPhone.

What the app does not do

  • NO ACCOUNTSThere's nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • NO NETWORKThe app makes no internet connections. All sixteen songs are inside it.
  • NO ANALYTICS, NO TRACKING, NO ADSWe don't measure how you play, and nothing is shared with advertisers or anyone else.
  • NO PERMISSIONSIt never asks for your microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location.

What stays on your iPhone

Your two settings

Whether callouts are on and whether the title card shows at launch. They're saved on your iPhone so the app remembers them, and nowhere else.

Your recordings

REC captures the app's own sound (not your microphone). When you tap stop, the take is handed to the iOS share sheet so you can choose where it goes. The app then deletes its temporary copies. Anything you save or send goes only where you send it.

Children

Mixtape Pachinko collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting everything

Delete the app and its settings go with it. There is nothing held on our side to delete.

Changes

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will change first, with a new date at the top.

This page covers Mixtape Pachinko. The policy for every Pipchi Productions app is at pipchi.com/privacy.
