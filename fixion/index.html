From the listening room

A story is better with someone in the room.

Short fiction is a strange thing to hand somebody. It's over quickly, and the good ones leave a mark you can't quite point at. So Fixion does it the long way — pick one story, sit with it a while, say what's actually happening underneath it, and then read the thing start to finish.

You can skip the talking. The story's always there at the end. But the talking is the point.
