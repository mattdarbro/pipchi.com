Four songs, one village, completely different meanings. The third door Dr. Wally Boffin opened.

Volume · III Songs · 4 Found by · Dr. Wally Boffin
From Dr. Wally Boffin

One village. Four songs. Four entirely different stories.

Gaslight and tape hiss. This door gave onto a Victorian village at night — cobblestones still wet, a carriage and an early motorcar sharing the lane, every window lit warm. Four songs came through, and here's the strange part: they're about the same little place, and not one of them agrees on what happened there.

That's the kind of universe I live for. Play them in order. Then play them again and watch the meanings switch.

— Dr. Wally Boffin, between dimensions
