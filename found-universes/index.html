From Dr. Wally Boffin

I don't invent them. I find them.

People think I make these places up. I don't. The doors were always there — I just got curious enough to open them, and patient enough to write down what came back through. A few songs. A loop of color. The rest is gone before I can catch it.

Every volume is one universe, the way I found it. When I open another door, it goes up here. Keep an eye on the archive.

— Dr. Wally Boffin, between dimensions
— with care, Matt
