Why "Save My Shorts"?

Little films get made and then they get lost — buried in a feed, never screened, never sat with. Pirou doesn't let that happen. Every short that comes through gets a marquee, a seat saved, and a reason to be watched on purpose. That's the whole job: save the shorts.

From Pirou

If it moved, it belongs on a screen. Sit down. I'll get the lights.
