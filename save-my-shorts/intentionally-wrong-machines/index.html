Pirou's note

The wrong machines dance better.

Down a gaslit alley under a sign that reads Mechanical Dreams, there's a crew with gears strapped to their bodies, sparks kicking up off the cobbles, one of them flipped clean upside-down mid-step. Nothing here runs to spec. That's the joke, and the joy.

This one's basically my anthem. Build it a little wrong on purpose and watch it move. Precision keeps time; the wrong machines keep the party.

— Pirou, gloriously off-spec
